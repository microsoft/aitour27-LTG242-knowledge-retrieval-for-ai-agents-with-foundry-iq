Event description and containment

Detection: In-process bioburden sample, vessel day 17. The isolate was identified as Ralstonia pickettii.

Immediate action: Run isolated; transfer path and hold tank placed on quality hold.

Evidence reviewed

  • Positive sample localized downstream of transfer connection TC-4
  • Retained upstream sample remained negative
  • Gasket inspection found a visible compression fold
  • Maintenance closeout omitted the required post-assembly pressure-hold result

Root cause and responsibility

A supplier-maintained transfer-hose connection was reassembled with a pinched gasket after preventive maintenance, compromising the closed path.

Sponsor-cause review: No sponsor material, instruction, or change contributed to the connection failure.

Root cause conclusion

A supplier-maintained transfer-hose connection was reassembled with a pinched gasket after preventive maintenance, compromising the closed path.

Impact assessment

The affected run remained isolated and no released product was impacted. The investigation is supplier-responsible.
