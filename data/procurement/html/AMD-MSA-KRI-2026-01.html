  1. Adds CALD-201 50 mg film-coated tablets to the product schedule.
  2. Reserves 420,000 tablets of monthly capacity for the 12-month firm supply period.
  3. Fixes commercial product pricing at USD 0.108 per released tablet and technology transfer at USD 295,000.
  4. Requires four weeks of finished-product safety stock after launch.
  5. Adds technology-transfer milestones and a target first PPQ date of 15 March 2027.
  6. Conditionally approves Piedmont Analytical Services only for compendial microbiology testing; further subcontracting requires written approval.

4. Quality and supply controls

Kristos shall not begin engineering, PPQ, or commercial execution until the applicable protocols and quality responsibilities are approved. The Quality Agreement governs deviation notification, OOS investigations, change control, data integrity, batch disposition, complaints, and recalls.

Piedmont Analytical Services is conditionally approved only for the stated testing scope. Kristos remains fully responsible for subcontracted work, data, investigations, and timely delivery. Any location, method, or scope change requires Caldova's prior written approval.

5. Unchanged terms

Except as expressly changed by this Amendment, MSA-CAL-KRI-2024-017 remains unchanged and in full force. If a conflict exists, this Amendment controls for CALD-201. The purchase order provides quantities and dates but does not alter prices or quality obligations.
