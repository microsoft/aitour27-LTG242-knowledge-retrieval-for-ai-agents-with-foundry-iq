1. Procurement scope

CALD-201 50 mg film-coated tablets is a fictional immediate-release product used to demonstrate retrieval across solicitation, bid, agreement, amendment, and order records. The successful supplier will provide technology transfer, GMP manufacture, testing, release, storage, and bulk preparation.

Required services

  • Reserve capacity for 400,000 tablets per month and ten 480,000-tablet batches per year.
  • Maintain at least four weeks of finished-product safety stock after commercial launch.
  • Complete method, process, cleaning, packaging, and documentation transfer before PPQ.
  • Manufacture, test, release, store, and prepare bulk tablets for Caldova-designated carriage.
  • Provide certificates of analysis and compliance, executed batch records, deviation packages, and annual product-quality data.
  • Do not subcontract GMP manufacturing or release testing without Caldova's prior written approval.

2. Evaluation framework

Qualification follows CAL-POL-PUR-001, version 1.0. Mandatory gates are applied before weighted award scoring. Lowest price alone does not determine award.

CategoryWeightEvidence considered
Technical and operational25%OSD experience, transfer method, process fit, documentation
Quality and regulatory25%Inspection history, audit status, quality systems, release controls
Capacity and supply20%Firm capacity, lead time, safety stock, continuity
Commercial20%Firm unit price, transfer fees, terms, total evaluated cost
Implementation and risk10%Milestones, dependencies, subcontractors, financial resilience

Mandatory gates

  • Quality approval and an executed quality agreement before GMP manufacture
  • No unresolved critical audit findings or disqualifying regulatory status
  • Overall supplier qualification score of at least 70 of 100
  • Acceptance of Net 60, USD pricing, four weeks of safety stock, and written subcontractor approval

Required response sections

  1. Signed bid letter and legal-entity details
  2. Technical approach and technology-transfer plan
  3. Quality, regulatory, inspection, and audit evidence
  4. Capacity reservation, continuity, and safety-stock plan
  5. Firm 12-month price schedule in USD
  6. Exceptions, assumptions, subcontractors, and implementation risks

Commercial baseline

  • Firm USD pricing for 12 months
  • Net 60 payment terms
  • At least four weeks of safety stock
  • Written approval before subcontracting
  • No commencement before written authorization

Caldova may clarify, negotiate, reject any or all proposals, or discontinue this fictional process. Bid information is confidential and used only for this demonstration corpus.
