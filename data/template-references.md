# Template references for the sourcing document set

These public documents are structural references for the synthetic Caldova artifacts. The generated documents should use fictional organizations, products, identifiers, prices, signatures, findings, and outcomes. Do not copy source branding, case facts, confidential redactions, or substantial passages of source language.

## Caldova sourcing policy

**Selected company model:** [PharmaMar Group Purchasing Policy](https://pharmamar.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/PHARMAMAR_PURCHASING_POLICY_v2.pdf), PharmaMar Group, 25 pages.

This is the closest company-specific match: a pharmaceutical group's internal policy for purchasing materials and equipment and contracting works and services. Use its structure for policy purpose and scope; the role of a specialized purchasing organization; segregation of requester and purchaser duties; supplier competition; transparent decisions and objective award criteria; security of supply, cost, quality, service, innovation, regulatory, and ESG considerations; approved supplier panels; criticality-based qualification; documented exceptions; expenditure approval; purchasing ethics; conflict-of-interest declarations; and a supply-chain code of conduct.

**Supplier lifecycle model:** [Cipla Limited Responsible Sourcing Policy](https://www.cipla.com/sites/default/files/2024-08/Responsible-Sourcing-Policy.pdf), issued 5 August 2024, 6 pages. Use its operational sequence of supplier classification, pre-onboarding assessment and scoring, critical-supplier evaluation, risk-based audits, corrective action plans, escalation or termination after unresolved deviations, training, and a non-retaliation grievance mechanism.

**Pharmaceutical quality benchmark:** [WHO Quality assurance policy for the procurement of essential medicines and other health products](https://www.who.int/publications-detail-redirect/9789240023789), World Health Organization, 4 June 2021, 20 pages, ISBN 978-92-4-002378-9. Use it only to check medicine-specific product and source quality requirements; it is no longer the structural policy template.

**Adaptation boundary:** both company documents are public policies, not reusable boilerplate. The Caldova policy will follow their issue coverage while using original language, fictional governance roles, and fictional thresholds. It will not reproduce company branding, contact details, declarations, or code-of-conduct text.

## RFP and three supplier responses

**Selected model:** [Procurement of Health Sector Goods: Pharmaceuticals, Vaccines, and Condoms](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0052c2e9d357685cde6e64e99b36aaed-0290032021/original/SBD-Procurement-of-Health-Sector-Goods-June-2021.docx), World Bank standard bidding document, June 2021.

The source is specifically designed for pharmaceutical procurement. Its structure includes:

- Part 1: bidding procedures.
- Instructions to bidders and a bid data sheet.
- Evaluation and qualification criteria.
- Bid submission and price-schedule forms.
- Part 2: supply requirements, including a schedule of requirements and technical specifications.
- Part 3: general and special conditions plus contract forms.

Use the solicitation-side sections for the Caldova RFP. Use the bidding forms, bid submission letter, qualification evidence, technical schedules, and price schedules as a common response framework for all three suppliers. Each response should have distinct branding, prose, commercial assumptions, risks, exceptions, and evidence while preserving comparable fields.

**Adaptation boundary:** retain the document architecture and field concepts, not World Bank instructions, legal language, logos, or project facts.

## Three manufacturer agreements

**Selected model:** [Long Term Manufacturing Agreement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609809/000095017022002435/mcrb-ex10_25.htm), Seres Therapeutics, Inc. and BacThera AG, effective 8 November 2021, filed as SEC Exhibit 10.25.

This is a real pharmaceutical contract-manufacturing agreement. Its verified sections cover definitions; facility; governance; personnel; customer and manufacturer obligations; purchase and supply; materials; manufacture, testing, acceptance, rejection, delivery, storage, and batch failure; intellectual property; subcontractors; regulatory and quality matters; charges and payment; confidentiality; warranties; indemnification; disputes; term and termination; insurance; and miscellaneous terms. Exhibits cover product specifications, supplied materials, charges, key personnel, technology transfer, and approved subcontractors.

Use [FDA's Contract Manufacturing Arrangements for Drugs: Quality Agreements](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/contract-manufacturing-arrangements-drugs-quality-agreements) as the quality-responsibility reference. Its verified quality-agreement topics include quality-unit activities, facilities and equipment, materials management, product-specific considerations, laboratory controls, documentation, and change control.

**Adaptation boundary:** SEC exhibits are public filings, not neutral legal templates. Use only their organization and issue coverage. Draft original, simplified fictional terms and do not reproduce redacted or deal-specific language. These artifacts are demo data, not legal advice.

## Contract amendment

**Selected model:** [Amendment to Long Term Manufacturing Agreement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609809/000095017023006374/mcrb-ex10_28.htm), Seres Therapeutics, Inc. and BacThera AG, dated 14 December 2022, filed as SEC Exhibit 10.28.

The source directly amends the manufacturing agreement selected above. Its compact structure includes parties and recitals; definitions; changed scope; capital-target adjustments; milestone-payment replacement; budget updates; preservation of unaffected terms; counterparts; signatures; and an attached change order.

Use GSA [Standard Form 30, Amendment of Solicitation/Modification of Contract](https://www.gsa.gov/forms-library/amendment-solicitationmodification-contract) as a visual/metadata reference for amendment number, effective date, underlying contract number and date, authority/type of change, description, unchanged-terms statement, and signatures.

**Adaptation boundary:** write original Caldova amendment language and use fictional business terms. Do not copy either source's parties, values, or clauses.

## Spanish-language quality certificate

**Content model:** [WHO model certificate of analysis](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/trs1010-annex4), WHO Technical Report Series 1010, Annex 4, 30 September 2018.

The WHO model is explicitly an example rather than a prescriptive form. Use its laboratory and customer details; certificate and sample identifiers; product/material name; batch and manufacturer; dates; specification and test-method references; a table of tests, acceptance criteria, and results; conclusion; comments; attachments; and authorized review/approval.

**Language presentation model:** [Information note on the format and validity features of electronic certificates for medicines issued by the European Medicines Agency](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/regulatory-procedural-guideline/information-note-format-and-validity-features-electronic-certificates-medicines-issued-european-medicines-agency_en.pdf), EMA/206719/2020 Rev. 2, updated 16 January 2023. Annex I contains an example EN-ES-FR-PT electronic certificate. EMA's [Certification of medicinal products](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/post-authorisation/certification-medicinal-products) page confirms that these certificates support authorities outside the EU and attest marketing-authorization and GMP status.

Use the WHO analytical table in a Spanish-only certificate issued by Meridian API Works Iberia's manufacturing and quality-control site in Spain. The Singapore entity remains the commercial supplier named on the English invoice. Shared certificate, lot, material, invoice, and yield-workbook identifiers create the cross-language retrieval link. Use EMA only as evidence that Spanish is an established certificate language, not as a visual or legal-status model.

**Adaptation boundary:** do not imitate an EMA electronic signature, verification mark, seal, or official status. The artifact must be conspicuously fictional in metadata while remaining realistic on the page.

## Scanned GMP report with a figure

**Selected model:** [Guidance on good manufacturing practices: inspection report](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/guidance-on-good-manufacturing-practices-inspection-report), WHO Technical Report Series 996, Annex 4, 31 October 2016, 16 pages.

Its model report has three verified parts:

- General information about the authority, site, inspection team, dates, products, and inspection history.
- Findings and recommendations organized across pharmaceutical quality, production, facilities/equipment, laboratory control, materials, packaging/labelling, sanitation, qualification/validation, complaints, recalls, contract production, self-inspection, personnel, training, hygiene, premises, equipment, materials, documentation, and good practices.
- Deficiencies classified as critical, major, or other, followed by conclusions and signatures.

The synthetic report should add one clearly captioned facility-flow or contamination-risk figure as an inspection attachment. That figure is a Caldova demo extension, not a feature copied from the WHO model. Render the final artifact as a deliberately imperfect scan so OCR and figure extraction are both meaningful.

**Adaptation boundary:** use the recommended report organization and deficiency taxonomy, but create all observations, classifications, facility details, and the figure from scratch.

## Purchase order

**Selected model:** [Order for Supplies or Services, GSA Form 3186](https://www.gsa.gov/forms-library/order-supplies-or-services), U.S. General Services Administration, revision 07/1994.

Use the form's compact order layout as a field reference: order and requisition identifiers; issue date; buyer and supplier; billing and delivery addresses; contract reference; line-item number, description, quantity, unit, unit price, and amount; delivery schedule and instructions; terms; totals; and authorization/acceptance.

The pharmaceutical agreement above is a domain-specific secondary source: its definition of a purchase order requires the number of batches, product, shipping instructions including container and temperature requirements, requested delivery dates, and delivery destinations. Include those details in the synthetic Caldova order.

**Adaptation boundary:** use original Caldova visual design and language rather than reproducing the federal form or implying a government order.

## Supplier network map

**Base map:** [WorldMap.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WorldMap.svg),
original by David Kernow and vectorized by CodeOne. Wikimedia Commons identifies
the Robinson-projection map as public domain because its source material came
from the United States Central Intelligence Agency's World Factbook. Marker
projection uses the map family's 10°E central meridian.

The supplier overlay is original Caldova demonstration content. Marker positions
are projected from source-backed billing cities, the explicit BluePeak inspected
site, Caldova purchase-order locations, or representative state centers where
the vendor source provides only a state. The map does not assert that billing
addresses are manufacturing sites and does not crosswalk the `V001`–`V007` and
`sup-001`–`sup-015` namespaces.
